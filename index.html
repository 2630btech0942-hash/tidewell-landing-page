Questions, answered

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. The free Tidewell app supports iOS 15+ and Android 10+.

Is the bottle dishwasher safe?

The steel body is top-rack safe. Remove the base sensor first, then rinse it by hand.

How do I charge it?

Use the included USB-C cable. A full charge lasts about 30 days.

What if I don’t like it?

Return it within 60 days for a full refund.
